# Data dictionary

CSV files use UTF-8 (with a BOM where generated by the original code). Decimal values retain analysis precision. Empty values indicate inapplicability, infeasible targets or deliberately withheld location fields, according to the column; they must not be read as zero. `True`/`False` are booleans. `strict` and `wide` are existing-meal-registry scenarios. `M` is meal assistance, `S` daytime activities, `H` primary care and `G` parks. `ALL16` means pooled residents of the 16 sampled circles.

## Frozen replication inputs

| File | Row grain and fields |
|---|---|
| `routing_origins.csv` | Origin × registry; `case_id`, `origin_id`, `scenario`, `older_population` (estimated residents aged 60+); `distance_*_m` (directed walking distance to nearest existing provider); `covered_*` (distance ≤720 m); `initial_deficit` (number of missing H/M/S/G functions). Infinity means no feasible route to a recorded provider. Origin XY coordinates are omitted. |
| `routing_candidates.csv` | Eligible candidate × function × registry; `site_id` (stable identifier), `case_id`, `pool_type` (`inside` station, `offsite` other venue), `source_family`, vintage/retrieval descriptors and `connector_m`. `site_name` repeats the stable ID; address and coordinate columns are intentionally empty. Zero-benefit candidates remain eligible. |
| `routing_reachable_od.csv` | Circle × origin × site, with actual shortest `distance_m` ≤720. Unlisted pairs do not qualify within the archived threshold. This table includes reachable pairs whether or not the origin was initially unserved. |
| `routing_gains.csv` | Circle × registry × function × eligible site × newly reachable origin; stores `older_population`, `initial_deficit`, `distance_m`. Union origins, rather than summing candidate gains, when evaluating multiple sites. |

## Primary results

| File | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `siting_budget_comparison.csv` | Registry × service × resident scope × site budget. `inside_only_*` and `expanded_*` report eligible counts, effective selected counts, maximum gained population and representative plan IDs. `same_number_feasible` distinguishes exactly K from at most K. |
| `siting_targets.csv` | Minimum sites for 1/5/10/20/30/50/75/100% of initially unserved population; `status`, target headcount, gain at minimum, attainable ceiling, shortfall and plan ID. |
| `siting_total_targets.csv` | Minimum sites for 50/60/70/80/90/95/100% coverage of all older residents; `already_achieved` means zero sites suffice. |
| `siting_ceiling.csv` | All-candidate upper bound, remaining uncovered population and minimum sites needed to attain the ceiling. |
| `siting_plan_sites.csv` | Representative `plan_id` × `site_id` membership and pool type. Names/addresses/coordinates are omitted. |
| `siting_pooled_distribution.csv` | Each pooled representative plan's gained population attributed to each resident circle. |
| `siting_operator_directory_sensitivity.csv` | Station plus operator-listed restaurant subset; gains at selected budgets, ceiling and selected site IDs. |
| `data/site_level_results.csv` | Each candidate's newly reachable origin count and weighted population, per registry and service. Individual site gains are not additive. |

## Supplemental results

`walking_thresholds.csv` records initial unserved population, its 5% target, minimum K, optimal gain at K−1 and K, ten-site gains and ceiling for each service/menu/speed combination. `menu` is `stations`, `operator` or `all`.

`meal_site_removals.csv` includes the no-removal reference (`removed_site_id=none`) and every one of 199 exclusions. It records minimum K, target, best one- and two-site gains, selected IDs and all tied best two-site combinations. It changes candidate availability while keeping existing-service baselines fixed.

`activity_plans.csv` describes `station_only`, `expanded`, `min_7_circles`, `min_8_circles` and `circle_floors`. It reports newly covered population, positive-gain circle count, selected count, gain above station-only and independent MILP optimum. `activity_circles.csv` gives each policy's local gains, reference gains and required floors.

`data/citywide_400_circle_access.csv` describes the separate mapped-residential-land denominator, not the whole-circle denominator of the 16-circle siting experiment. `has_mapped_denominator=False` identifies 126 unevaluable circles; they are not zero-access observations. Population sums and service-access numerators are supplied so pooled percentages can be recomputed.
